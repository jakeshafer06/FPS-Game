        Overnight rules. Read these every night.

        Kettle Creek Gas & Go · Rt 41

        1. Every hour, log the store in the logbook by the register before the hour runs out. ALL CLEAR if nothing is different. REPORT if something is, and say where.
        2. Walk the whole store before you log. Aisles, coolers, coffee, the back room, the office.
        3. A wrong log, a missed hour, or a broken rule is a strike. Three strikes and you're done for the night.
        4. Serve every customer. Ring up what they bring, get them what they ask for, and count their change right.
        5. Real customers come in the front door, and the bell rings. It's pouring, so they're soaked and dripping. They blink. They sound normal.
        6. If someone is bone dry, grey in the face, or their voice sounds wrong, it is not a customer. Same for anyone who comes in twice, comes out of the back, or asks for something we don't sell.
        7. If a customer is wrong, refuse service. Don't ring it up.
        8. If someone is standing in the store who didn't come in the front door, don't look away from him.
        9. Don't open the back door if someone is knocking.
        10. If the lights go out, get in the office and shut the door. Stay there until they come back on.
        11. If everything goes quiet, don't move until the sound comes back.
        12. Don't stay outside long. If the back room light is out, don't stay back there long.
        13. Customers come first. Serve everyone who comes in before you log the hour.
        14. Three strikes and the store locks down. Get the keys from the register, get the power back on in the office, and get out the front door. Don't let it see you. Crouch behind the shelves.
        15. Before every shift, do your chores, then clock in at the register.
        16. Keep the generator fed and the back door bolted. Spare flashlight batteries are in the back room.
        17. I will never call you after 2 AM. Ever.
        18. If something is hunting you, crouch behind the shelves or shut yourself in the office until it gives up. If it catches you, that's it.
        19. Sometimes the store gets lost. Doors open onto the wrong places. Aisles get longer. Walk it off. That doesn't count as an anomaly.
        20. Noises come and go. So do things you see outside for a second. Only report what stays changed.
